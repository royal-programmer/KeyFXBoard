Key FX Board

Type anywhere. A click, a custom sample, or a piano — with a real FX rack. Nothing you type is recorded.

After install

  • The app lives in the system tray. Double-click the tray icon to open the window; a single click mutes.
  • First launch may take a few seconds while factory piano samples are written to AppData.
  • Mute, volume, and output device are on Home. Profiles and effects are saved setups, not extra apps.

Sounds

  • Profiles pick a pack and a room (Dry, Reverb, Bass, Surround).
  • Custom sample — drop files in the custom folder, then choose the file on Profiles.
  • Piano — Instruments → Start. Closing the map does not stop piano; Stop does.

Where files live

Settings and your packs stay in %AppData%\KeyFXBoard. Uninstalling the program does not have to delete that folder. Use Settings → Remove local data if you want a clean wipe.

App updates (new Instruments, later versions) replace the program under %LocalAppData%\KeyFXBoard. Your profiles stay.

Privacy

A global keyboard hook exists only to play a sound. We do not log keys, window titles, or what you type.
