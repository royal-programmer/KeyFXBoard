# You're ready

Key FX Board is installed for your Windows user account.

- Open it from the **Start menu** or the **desktop shortcut**
- After the first-run welcome, a thank-you page may open in your browser with quick tips
- Getting started: https://royal-programmer.github.io/KeyFXBoard/getting-started.html
- Product page & downloads: https://royal-programmer.github.io/KeyFXBoard/
- New versions (including instruments and fixes) install through **Settings → Check for updates**

Click **Finish**. The app can launch when setup completes.
